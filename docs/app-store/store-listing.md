🌟 核心技術亮點
• 全自動硬體規格識別（ISO 14443-4 / ISO 7816 APDU）

• 100% 純離線隱私保障
  所有影像裁切與抖動運算均在 iPhone 本地端完成，零網路連線、零廣告、零追蹤。

## 4. Apple App Review 審核員專用說明 (Notes for App Review)

```text
【Hardware & Demo Mode Instructions for Apple App Review】

1. Purpose of CoreNFC (ISO 7816):
   TapInk updates passive (battery-free) NFC E-Paper / E-Ink displays (such as Santek EZ Sign 2.9" 2-Color and 4.2" 4-Color displays) via ISO 14443-4 / ISO 7816 APDU commands (`NFCISO7816Tag`).

2. Built-in Hardware-Free Demo Mode (For Reviewers Without Physical NFC E-Paper Hardware):
   If you do not have a physical passive NFC E-Paper tag on hand during review, you can verify the complete end-to-end 4-color Floyd-Steinberg dithering, LZO1X compression, and APDU state machine directly inside the app:
   - Step 1: Launch TapInk (a sample 4-color artwork is preloaded, or pick any photo via the Photos button).
   - Step 2: LONG-PRESS (press and hold for 1.0 second) directly on the central E-Paper Preview Card.
   - Step 3: The built-in `SimulatedIsoDepTransceiver` will execute a full simulated 4.2" 400×300 4-Color (BWRY) NFC transfer (1% -> 100% progress, 30,000-byte hardware bit-packing, and completion checkmark).

3. Physical iPhone NFC Antenna Placement Note (If Testing With Real Hardware):
   Passive E-Paper tags harvest all operating & screen-refresh power from the iPhone's NFC field:
   - Align the VERY TOP EDGE of the back of the iPhone (next to the camera module) flat against the center antenna area of the E-Paper tag.
   - Remove thick or metal/MagSafe wallet cases if the RF coupling is weak, and keep the iPhone completely still until 100% physical refresh completes.
```
